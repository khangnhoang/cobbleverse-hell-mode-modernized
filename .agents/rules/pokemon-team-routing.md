# Pokémon Team Design Routing

When a task involves creating, modernizing, redesigning, or reviewing competitive NPC Pokémon Doubles teams in this repository, activate and follow the workspace skill:

- `competitive-pokemon-doubles-team-design` (`.agents/skills/competitive-pokemon-doubles-team-design/SKILL.md`)

All design methodology, Doubles synergies, gimmick safety rules, and validation checklists are defined in that skill.
